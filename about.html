THE BOXER AND HIS JOURNEY

Getting to Know Ippo

Ippo Makunouchi begins as a quiet student who helps with his family's fishing business. Boxing gives him a new challenge and a chance to learn what strength means to him.

His Boxing Journey

Ippo trains at the Kamogawa Boxing Gym and builds his skills step by step. He faces tough opponents, but he also has to manage nerves, mistakes, and pressure. His journey shows that effort matters, even when winning is not easy.

The People Around Him

Coach Kamogawa

His coach teaches him boxing basics, discipline, and how to prepare for a match.

His Gym Friends

Other boxers give Ippo people to learn from, compete with, and support.

His Opponents

Each opponent challenges him in a different way and helps him understand his own limits.
